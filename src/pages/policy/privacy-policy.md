## 1. Introduction
Welcome to businesstools. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

By using our platform, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

### (a) Personal Information
When you create an account, process payments, or contact us, we may collect your:

- Name
- Email

- Payment details

We collect IP addresses for rate limiting and security purposes.

Additionally, cookies and similar tracking technologies are used for analytics and website functionality.

Any content uploaded by users remains the property of the user.

However, users are responsible for ensuring their content complies with all applicable laws. The user will be responsible for all legal implications of their content.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain our services
- Authenticate users (via Stripe)
- Process payments (via Stripe and Razorpay)
- Send emails, notifications, and updates (via Resend)
- Improve security through rate limiting based on IP addresses
- Analyze user behavior using cookies to enhance user experience
- Communicate updates via email

## 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services
We do not sell or rent your data. However, we may share data with:

- **Stripe & Razorpay** — Payment processing
- **Stripe** — Authentication
- **Resend** — Email communication
- **Analytics providers** — Usage tracking through cookies
- **Hosting services** — Vercel for frontend and Hetzner for VPS backend

These third-party services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

## 5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies for analytics and improving user experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.
