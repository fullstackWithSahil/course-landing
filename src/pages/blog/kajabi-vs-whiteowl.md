## Website Design & Performance: We Build It For You

Your website is your storefront. With Kajabi, you manage the build. With WhiteOwl, we build it *for* you.

### Kajabi's Approach: Self-Service Builder

Kajabi provides a drag-and-drop website builder with templates. It's flexible, but that flexibility comes at a cost:

- **Learning curve** — you have to invest time learning the builder, time that's taken away from creating content

- **Performance hit** — no-code builders often produce slower, unoptimized code; many Kajabi sites score between **40–60** on Google PageSpeed Insights, which directly hurts SEO and the visitor experience

- **Generic look** — even with customization options, sites built from the same blocks and templates tend to look similar to each other

### WhiteOwl's Approach: Custom, Hand-Coded Development

Every WhiteOwl customer gets a dedicated professional web designer and developer — at no additional cost.

- **Design & approval** — our designer creates custom mockups based on your brand, and you review and refine until it's exactly right

- **Development** — our developer hand-codes your site so it's clean, fast, and genuinely unique

**The results:**

- **Zero learning curve** — focus entirely on your course material

- **Superior performance** — most of our hand-coded sites score 100% on Google PageSpeed Insights, and we guarantee a score above 90%

- **True uniqueness** — need custom animations, interactive elements, or specific functionality? We build it. No templates, no blocks required.
