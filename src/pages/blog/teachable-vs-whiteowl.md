Starting an online course business is hard. It's not just about a website and a video player — you also need to set up workspaces, integrate payment providers, get approved by Google, configure Google Search Console, write legal pages, and handle a dozen other details most course creators never anticipated.

Teachable and WhiteOwl take fundamentally different approaches to solving this problem. **Teachable is software-as-a-service.** **WhiteOwl is software backed by consultancy-as-a-service.**

Teachable hands you a course-hosting tool and a template builder, then leaves you to figure out the rest on your own. WhiteOwl does the opposite: we set up everything we can, and we guide you personally through everything we can't. Need strategy advice, a technical setup unrelated to our software, or legal pages like a terms of service, privacy policy, or refund policy? Just ask — it's part of what we do.

Here's a closer look at how the two platforms compare.

## Website Design & Performance: We Build It For You

Your website is your storefront. With Teachable, you manage the build inside a fixed template. With WhiteOwl, we build it *for* you.

### Teachable's Approach: Template-Based Builder

Teachable's course and sales pages are built with a block-based editor, and creators consistently note the same trade-off: it's easy to get started, but the layout follows a fixed structure with limited flexibility to truly match your brand. It's not a full website builder — it's a course-hosting tool with a page editor bolted on. That's fine for a fast, no-frills launch, but it also means:

- **Rigid layouts** — you can add and rearrange blocks, but you're working within a fixed structure, not a custom design
- **Generic look** — because everyone is building from the same block library, course pages across different creators tend to look similar
- **Time trade-off** — customizing what you can still takes time away from creating content, with a lower ceiling on what's possible

### WhiteOwl's Approach: Custom, Hand-Coded Development

Every WhiteOwl customer gets a dedicated professional web designer and developer — at no additional cost.

1. **Design & approval** — our designer creates custom mockups based on your brand, and you review and refine until it's exactly right
2. **Development** — our developer hand-codes your site so it's clean, fast, and genuinely unique

**The results:**

- **Zero learning curve** — focus entirely on your course material
- **Superior performance** — most of our hand-coded sites score 100% on Google PageSpeed Insights, and we guarantee a score above 90%
- **True uniqueness** — need custom animations, interactive elements, or specific functionality? We build it. No templates, no blocks required.

WhiteOwl doesn't gate core functionality behind pricing tiers like this. You get unlimited email templates, unlimited API calls, and unlimited MCP tool calls from day one — no separate "unlock" required as you grow.

## Community Features: Built In, Not Bolted On

Building a community around your course drives engagement, boosts completion rates, and increases lifetime customer value — so it shouldn't be an afterthought.

**Teachable has no native community feature at all.** The closest thing on the platform is lecture-level commenting. To add a real community — group chat, discussion spaces, live events — Teachable points creators to a native integration with Circle.so, a separate platform that starts at roughly **$99/month on its own**, stacked on top of your Teachable subscription.

**WhiteOwl includes a group chat community free with every course**, organized into topic-based channels, and it doesn't count toward your product limit. Need more than one community, or want a standalone community without a course attached? That's a $20/month add-on — not a $99+/month separate product.

WhiteOwl was built from the ground up for the age of AI. API and MCP access aren't an upsell; they're a core part of every plan. **Anything you can do through our UI, you can also do through our API — or hand off entirely to an AI using MCP.**

## Live Sessions & Webinars: A Level Playing Field

This is one area where the two platforms are closer than you might expect.

**Teachable** has no native webinar or live-streaming feature either. To run a live session, you embed a third-party tool — YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch, or a Zoom link — into a lesson.

**WhiteOwl** takes the same approach today: live sessions integrate with Zoom, Google Meet, or similar tools, with native in-platform live sessions on our development roadmap.

If frequent, fully native live sessions are a must-have for you right now, neither platform delivers that out of the box — you'll want to budget for a dedicated webinar tool either way.
